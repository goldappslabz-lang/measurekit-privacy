Measure Kit Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026 · Developer: Goldappslabz · Contact: goldappslabz@gmail.com

What the app does

Measure Kit (package com.globalapps.measurekit) provides offline measuring tools: bubble level, ruler, protractor, sound meter and compass. It needs no account, and we don't run our own servers.

Data the app itself uses

  • Motion and magnetic sensors (level, compass): read on your device only while the tool is open. Never stored or transmitted.
  • Microphone (sound meter, only after you grant permission): audio is analysed on your device to compute a loudness value. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Settings (calibration values, unit choice): stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, app interaction data and diagnostic data.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy & ad choices on the home screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Data retention and deletion

We hold none of your data. Uninstalling the app deletes all settings stored on your device.
